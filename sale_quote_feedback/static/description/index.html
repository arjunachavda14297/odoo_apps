Automatic Sales Follow-up Odoo Apps
 
This Odoo app finds out why customers do not accept your quotations. When a quotation stays unanswered (or is rejected online), a short survey is sent automatically to the customer. The answers come back to the sale order, so your sales team can improve prices, delivery times and offers using real feedback.
 
 
Ask. Learn. Win more deals.
Features
Key Features
 
Everything you need to learn from quotations that did not turn into orders.
Automatic survey
Sent when a quotation gets no answer after the delay you choose (7 days by default).
Online rejection
Customers who reject the quotation in the portal also receive the survey.
Manual request
A Request Feedback button lets the salesperson ask for feedback at any time.
Answers on the sale order
Smart button, Customer Feedback tab and a chatter note with every answer.
Any survey, any question
Use the supplied survey or your own. Every answer is stored, even for new questions.
Customer Feedback Control
Administrators can enable or disable feedback requests for individual customers using a simple Boolean setting, giving them full control over who can receive feedback requests.
Workflow
How It Works
 
1
Send the quotation
As usual. Each send starts (or restarts) the countdown.
2
Wait for an answer
No reply after the delay, or an online rejection by the customer.
3
Survey is emailed
A personal one-time link. No login needed.
4
Answers come back
Stored on the quotation, with a note for the salesperson.

Included
Ready-to-Use Survey Included
 
Six useful questions are installed with the module. Edit them freely or select your own survey.
1
What is the main reason you did not move forward with our quotation? (price, competitor, delivery time, product fit, terms, project postponed, communication, other)
2
Which supplier did you choose? (shown only when the customer chose another supplier)
3
How would you rate our quotation? (1 to 5)
4
Would you consider us again for a future need?
5
What could we have done better?
6
May we contact you to talk about your feedback?
Reliable
Smart Handling of Real-Life Cases
 
Quotation revised and sent again
The countdown restarts and the old unanswered survey link is cancelled.
Set back to draft
Everything is reset, as if the quotation was never sent.
Quotation confirmed
Pending surveys are cancelled automatically: the deal is won.
Customer opt-out
A checkbox on the contact form stops all feedback requests to that customer or company.
One link, one answer
Each request has its own secure link. A survey attempts limit applies to that link only, never to the customer overall.
Expiring links
Unanswered requests expire after the validity you choose.
Security
Secure by Design
 
Own records only
Salespeople see the feedback of their own quotations. Sales managers and users with all-documents access see everything.
Multi-company
Settings and record rules are applied per company.
No survey rights needed
Answers are copied to the sale order, so sales users never need access to the Survey app.
Help
FAQs
 
Can I use this module with Odoo Online?
This app can not be used with Odoo Online (SaaS). It works on Odoo.sh and on-premise installations.
Does it send surveys to my existing quotations?
No. The feature is off by default. Quotations are only considered after you enable it and after they are sent again.
Do my salespeople need access to the Survey app?
No. The answers are copied to the sale order, so sales users only need their normal Sales access.
Can I change the questions or use my own survey?
Yes. Add or change questions, or select another survey in the settings. Every answer is stored.
How can I install the module?
Copy the module folder into your addons path, activate the developer mode, go to Apps > Update Apps List, then install Sales Quotation Feedback.
The module does not work, what should I do?
Please contact our support with a description of the problem and your Odoo version.
